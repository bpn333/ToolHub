# Mendeley

**Mendeley** is a reference management tool and academic social network that helps researchers organize their library of papers, generate citations, and discover related work. Owned by Elsevier, it combines a desktop and web-based reference manager with a research-focused social platform, letting users build a personal PDF library while also connecting with the broader academic community around shared research interests.

Like other reference managers, Mendeley automatically extracts citation details from PDFs and integrates directly with word processors to insert formatted citations while writing.

---

## 🌟 High-Level Capabilities

- **Reference Management:** Organize research papers and citation metadata in a searchable library.
- **PDF Annotation:** Highlight, note, and search full-text PDFs within your library.
- **Citation Generation:** Insert and format citations directly into Word documents.
- **Cloud Sync:** Access your reference library across desktop, web, and mobile.
- **Research Discovery:** Get paper recommendations based on your library and reading history.
- **Academic Networking:** Connect with researchers and follow work in your field.

---

## 📚 What It Helps You Do

Graduate students use Mendeley to build and organize a growing library of sources throughout a thesis or research project.

Researchers writing journal articles use its Word plugin to insert accurately formatted citations without manual reference list building.

Academics use Mendeley's discovery features to find relevant new papers based on their existing library and research interests.

Research teams share group libraries to keep collaborators aligned on the same set of sources during a joint project.

---

## 🚀 Why Mendeley Stands Out

Mendeley combines reference management with a genuine research discovery and networking layer, going beyond pure citation tooling into helping researchers find relevant new work.

Its PDF reader and annotation tools are tightly integrated with the reference library, so notes and highlights stay attached to the exact source they came from.

Being backed by Elsevier gives it deep integration with major academic databases and publisher metadata, which can make citation details unusually accurate and complete.

---

## 🧩 A Complete Research Management Toolkit

Mendeley supports the full research and writing workflow:

- Automatic metadata extraction from imported PDFs
- Full-text PDF search and annotation
- Word and LibreOffice citation plugins
- Thousands of supported citation styles
- Group libraries for collaborative projects
- Cross-device cloud syncing
- Personalized paper recommendations
- A public researcher profile and academic network

This combination of organization, writing support, and discovery is why many researchers keep Mendeley open throughout their entire research process.

---

## 📦 Common Workflows

- **Literature Review:** Collect, annotate, and organize sources while researching a topic.
- **Academic Writing:** Insert accurately formatted citations while drafting papers.
- **Collaborative Research:** Share group libraries with co-authors and research teams.
- **Research Discovery:** Find new relevant papers based on your existing library.
- **PDF Organization:** Maintain a searchable, annotated archive of research papers.

---

## 💡 Tips for Getting Started

- Install the browser and Word plugins early to streamline saving sources and citing them.
- Use folders and tags together to organize a growing library by project and topic.
- Take advantage of PDF annotation so notes stay linked to their original source.
- Set up a group library at the start of any collaborative research project.
- Regularly sync your library if working across multiple devices.

---

## 🧠 Who It Is Great For

Mendeley is well suited for students, researchers, and academics who want reference management combined with tools for discovering and connecting around research.

Its blend of citation management, PDF organization, and academic networking has made it a popular choice across universities and research institutions worldwide.
