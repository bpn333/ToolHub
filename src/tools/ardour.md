# Ardour

**Ardour** is a free and open-source digital audio workstation (DAW) built for recording, editing, mixing, and mastering audio at a professional level. It's used by musicians, podcasters, sound engineers, and film post-production teams who want the multi-track capabilities of commercial DAWs like Pro Tools or Logic Pro, without licensing costs or vendor lock-in.

Ardour runs natively on Linux, macOS, and Windows, and its development is funded largely through user subscriptions and donations rather than corporate ownership — a model that has kept it independent and community-driven for over two decades.

---

## 🌟 High-Level Capabilities

- **Multi-Track Recording:** Record and manage large numbers of audio and MIDI tracks simultaneously.
- **Non-Destructive Editing:** Edit audio without permanently altering original recordings.
- **Mixing:** Route signals through a flexible, unlimited-channel mixing console.
- **Plugin Support:** Use LV2, VST, VST3, and AU plugins for effects and instruments.
- **MIDI Sequencing:** Compose and edit MIDI alongside recorded audio.
- **Automation:** Automate volume, panning, and plugin parameters over time.

---

## 🎚️ What It Helps You Do

Musicians and bands use Ardour to record and produce full albums, from tracking individual instruments to final mastering.

Podcasters and voice-over artists edit and clean up multi-track dialogue recordings before publishing.

Film and game audio teams use Ardour for dialogue editing, sound design, and mixing alongside video reference.

Live sound engineers use its recording capabilities to capture and later mix multi-track live performances.

---

## 🚀 Why Ardour Stands Out

Ardour is a genuinely professional-grade DAW that's free and open-source, giving independent musicians and studios full production capability without a subscription.

Because it's cross-platform and plugin-format agnostic (supporting LV2, VST, VST3, and AU), it fits into existing workflows built around other tools rather than forcing a closed ecosystem.

Its transparent, community-funded development model means the roadmap is shaped by users rather than a single company's commercial priorities.

---

## 🧩 A Complete Production Toolkit

Ardour covers the full audio production pipeline:

- Unlimited audio and MIDI track recording
- Non-destructive, non-linear editing
- Flexible bus routing and mixing console
- Built-in and third-party plugin support
- MIDI editing and virtual instrument hosting
- Automation lanes for dynamic mixing
- Video timeline sync for post-production
- Export and mastering tools

This makes it capable of handling everything from a simple podcast edit to a full multi-track album mix.

---

## 📦 Common Workflows

- **Music Production:** Record, edit, and mix multi-track songs from demo to final master.
- **Podcast Editing:** Clean up, arrange, and export multi-speaker recordings.
- **Film & Game Audio:** Sync dialogue and sound design to picture.
- **Live Recording:** Capture multi-track live performances for later mixdown.
- **Mastering:** Apply final processing and loudness adjustments before release.

---

## 💡 Tips for Getting Started

- Learn Ardour's region-based editing model before diving into advanced automation.
- Use buses to group related tracks (e.g., drums, vocals) for easier mixing.
- Explore free LV2 plugin suites like Calf or x42 to expand effects options.
- Save session templates for recurring project types like podcasts or band recordings.
- Back up sessions regularly, since audio projects can grow large and complex quickly.

---

## 🧠 Who It Is Great For

Ardour is ideal for musicians, podcasters, audio engineers, and post-production teams who want a full-featured DAW without recurring subscription costs.

Its professional feature set and cross-platform availability have made it a respected alternative to commercial DAWs in both hobbyist and professional audio production.
