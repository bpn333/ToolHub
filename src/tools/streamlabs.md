# Streamlabs

**Streamlabs** is a livestreaming and broadcasting platform built on top of OBS Studio's open-source engine, adding a more approachable interface along with creator-focused features like alerts, overlays, widgets, and built-in monetization tools. It's widely used by streamers on Twitch, YouTube, and other platforms who want a more guided setup experience than raw OBS provides.

Beyond the desktop broadcasting app, Streamlabs offers a broader ecosystem of tools — cloud-based alerts, mobile apps, and analytics — aimed at helping creators manage their entire streaming presence, not just the broadcast itself.

---

## 🌟 High-Level Capabilities

- **Live Streaming:** Broadcast to Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, and other platforms simultaneously.
- **Overlays & Themes:** Apply pre-built or custom visual overlays to a stream.
- **Alerts & Widgets:** Show real-time donation, follower, and subscriber alerts on screen.
- **Scene Management:** Switch between different camera, game, and screen layouts.
- **Chat Integration:** Display and interact with viewer chat directly in the streaming interface.
- **Mobile Streaming:** Stream directly from a phone using the Streamlabs mobile app.

---

## 🎥 What It Helps You Do

New streamers use Streamlabs' guided setup and built-in themes to get a professional-looking stream running quickly, without manually configuring scenes and sources.

Established creators use custom overlays, alert boxes, and widgets to build a distinct visual brand around their stream.

Multi-platform broadcasters use Streamlabs to simultaneously stream to Twitch, YouTube, and other services from a single setup.

Mobile-first creators use the Streamlabs mobile app to stream gameplay or live events directly from a phone.

---

## 🚀 Why Streamlabs Stands Out

Streamlabs lowers the barrier to entry for livestreaming by bundling alerts, overlays, and widgets that would otherwise require separate tools or custom coding to set up with plain OBS.

Its marketplace of themes and overlays lets creators achieve a polished, branded look quickly, which matters for building an audience in a crowded streaming landscape.

Being built on the same open-source engine as OBS Studio means it retains solid streaming performance while adding a more creator-friendly layer on top.

Streamlabs bundles tools across the entire streaming experience:

- Mobile streaming app for on-the-go broadcasts

This bundling is why many creators choose Streamlabs over configuring similar functionality manually in other tools.

---

## 💡 Tips for Getting Started

- Use the built-in themes to get a working setup before customizing overlays from scratch.
- Test alert and widget placement so they don't obscure important gameplay or content.
- Keep scene layouts simple at first — added complexity can wait until you're comfortable switching scenes live.
- Monitor stream health settings (bitrate, resolution) to match your internet upload speed.
- Explore the chatbot tools early to help manage moderation as an audience grows.

## 🧠 Who It Is Great For

Streamlabs is a strong fit for new and growing streamers who want a guided, feature-rich broadcasting setup without manually assembling every overlay and alert system.

Its creator-focused ecosystem has made it one of the most popular entry points into livestreaming on platforms like Twitch and YouTube.
