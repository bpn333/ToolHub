# Zotero

**Zotero** is a free and open-source reference management tool that helps researchers, students, and academics collect, organize, cite, and share sources. It can automatically grab bibliographic details from a webpage, PDF, or library catalog with a single click, then insert properly formatted citations and bibliographies directly into word processors like Microsoft Word or Google Docs.

Built by academics for academics, Zotero has become a staple tool for anyone writing research papers, theses, or dissertations that require careful citation management.

---

## 🌟 High-Level Capabilities

- **Source Collection:** Save references from websites, library catalogs, and PDFs with one click.
- **Organization:** Sort sources into collections, tags, and sub-collections.
- **Citation Generation:** Automatically format citations in thousands of citation styles.
- **Word Processor Integration:** Insert citations and bibliographies directly while writing.
- **PDF Management:** Store, annotate, and search full-text PDFs alongside references.
- **Syncing & Sharing:** Sync libraries across devices and collaborate in shared group libraries.

---

## 📚 What It Helps You Do

Graduate students use Zotero to manage hundreds of sources across a thesis or dissertation without losing track of citation details.

Researchers collaborating on a paper share a group library so every co-author works from the same, up-to-date reference list.

Undergraduates use the browser connector to save sources while researching online, then generate a bibliography in seconds when the paper is due.

Academics annotate PDFs directly inside Zotero, keeping notes attached to the exact source they came from.

---

## 🚀 Why Zotero Stands Out

Zotero is free and open-source, with no subscription fees, in contrast to some competing reference managers that gate features behind paid tiers.

Its browser connector can detect and save bibliographic metadata from most academic databases, library catalogs, and even ordinary web pages automatically, removing tedious manual data entry.

Support for thousands of citation styles — including APA, MLA, Chicago, and journal-specific formats — means switching a paper's required style is often a matter of a few clicks rather than manual reformatting.

---

## 🧩 A Complete Research Toolkit

Zotero supports the full lifecycle of academic research:

- One-click source capture via browser connector
- Collections, sub-collections, and tags for organization
- Full-text PDF storage, search, and annotation
- Word and Google Docs plugins for in-text citations
- Thousands of built-in and downloadable citation styles
- Group libraries for collaborative research projects
- Cross-device syncing via Zotero's free cloud storage tier
- Plugins for advanced workflows like note-taking and AI summarization

This scope is why Zotero often becomes the backbone of a researcher's entire workflow, not just a citation add-on.

## 📦 Common Workflows

- **Literature Review:** Collect and organize sources while researching a topic.
- **Thesis & Dissertation Writing:** Manage large bibliographies across long documents.
- **Collaborative Research:** Share group libraries with co-authors and research teams.
- **Citation Formatting:** Generate accurate in-text citations and bibliographies automatically.
- **PDF Annotation:** Highlight and note key passages directly within saved PDFs.
- **Systematic Reviews:** Track and tag large numbers of sources by inclusion criteria.

---

## 💡 Tips for Getting Started

- Install the browser connector first — it's the fastest way to build a library.
- Use tags and collections together; tags cut across topics while collections organize by project.
- Set up the Word or Google Docs plugin early so citations stay accurate while writing.
- Create a group library for any collaborative research project from the start.
- Regularly sync your library if working across multiple devices.
- Explore citation style search to quickly find journal-specific formatting requirements.

---

## 🧠 Who It Is Great For

Zotero is ideal for students, graduate researchers, academics, and anyone writing documents that require careful, consistent citation management.

Its free, open-source model combined with deep integration into the research and writing process has made it one of the most trusted reference managers in academia.
